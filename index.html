13 NOVEMBER · YOUR SPECIAL DAY

To My Cutie Bobbyyyy,

Happy Birthday, babyyyy! ❤️

I honestly don't know where to start because there are so many things I want to tell you, and sometimes even words aren't enough to explain how much you mean to me.

First of all, I hope you know how special you are. You are one of the most caring people in my life. You always help me, support me, listen to me and somehow find ways to make things easier for me. You might not even realise how much those little things mean to me, but trust me, I notice every single one of them.

Of course, you're not always an angel. Sometimes you make me so angry that I decide not to talk to you for an entire day. 😂 But what makes me love you even more is that you don't get angry with me easily. You still handle my moods, my silly fights and my little tantrums. I know I can be difficult sometimes, but thank you for being patient with me.

And can we talk about how you look so strong and mature from the outside, but deep inside you're just my cutest, tiniest little baby? My Bobby babyyyy! 🥺 I love that side of you so much. I hope you never feel like you have to hide that soft side of yourself with me.

There's something else I want you to know...

I know you've faced struggles in your life. I wasn't there when you were going through those difficult moments, and I wish I could have been. But now that I know you, I don't want to be someone who is there only when everything is going well.

I want to be there when you're happy, when you're stressed, when things work out, and especially when nothing seems to be working out. ❤️

You don't always have to be strong, baby. You don't have to figure out everything alone. You have me too. I want to celebrate your victories, listen to your worries, comfort you on your bad days and remind you how amazing you are whenever you forget. I don't want to leave you alone when life gets difficult.

And look at us! Who would have thought that two people would connect so unexpectedly around this time last year? We didn't plan any of this, but somehow we found each other. And now, here we are, celebrating your birthday and almost one year of knowing each other.

I don't know what exactly life has planned for us, but I know what I wish for. I want more birthdays together, more silly conversations, more random fights, more laughter, more memories and more years of choosing each other.

I want this little unexpected connection of ours to become something that lasts forever. Distance might keep us away physically right now, but it doesn't change how much you mean to me.

Also, thank you for my birthday watch, babyyy. It genuinely means a lot to me. Every time I look at it, I'll remember that there's someone far away who thought of me and made my birthday special. I couldn't give you something like that right now, but I hope these words make you feel even a little of the love I have for you.

If there's one thing I want you to remember today, it's this: You are loved, you are appreciated, you are important, and you will always have a special place in my heart.

Keep smiling, keep growing, keep chasing everything you dream of. I wish you success, happiness, good health and all the beautiful things this world has to offer.

And please remember that no matter how old you get, you'll always be my cute little Bobby baby. No promotions from baby status allowed. 😂❤️

Thank you for coming into my life unexpectedly and making it so much more special. I hope this is just the beginning of our forever.

💗

I love youuu, my babyyyy!

Happy Birthday once again, my favourite human.

Forever yours,
Your annoying little girl 💕

One Last Thing...

Out of all the unexpected things that happened in my life, meeting you was one of my favourites.

I hope we keep finding each other, in every little moment, through every high and low.

Here's to us, baby. Always. ♾️
